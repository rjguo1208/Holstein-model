# 1D Holstein model with one attractive on-site defect

The first implementation changes only the electron potential, `V_i = -U δ_i0`.
It retains uniform nearest-neighbor hopping, phonon frequency and electron–phonon
coupling. Units are `t = omega = hbar = a = 1`; the calculations use
`lambda = g^2/(2 t omega) = 0.25, 0.5` and `U = 0.5, 1, 2`.
The state contains one electron at zero temperature. `U > 0` means attraction.

## Expansion and observables

`src/defect.hpp` implements continuous-time direct-space bare DiagMC. The
diagonal part is `H0 = omega Nph - U n0`; both electron hopping and electron–phonon
vertices are expanded. This differs from the existing fixed-momentum code,
which sums free hopping into the electron propagator. No interpolation of a
tabulated electron propagator is required. The direct-space expansion follows
the construction used by Mishchenko et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2483.
That paper studied a three-dimensional lattice; its coupling normalization and
critical impurity strength are not one-dimensional reference numbers.

The positive vacuum propagator is

`G_ii(tau) = <vac| c_i exp[-(H-mu) tau] c_i^dagger |vac>`.

A configuration contains a continuous-time electron path starting and ending
at site `i`, an even number `Nh` of nearest-neighbor hops, and `n` phonon arcs.
Every arc joins two vertices at the same lattice site. All pairings, including
crossed and nested arcs, are allowed. In the ordered-time measure its weight is

`t^Nh g^(2n) exp[U T0 - omega sum(v-u) + mu tau]`,

where `T0` is the residence time at the defect. Paths live on the infinite
integer chain. `--radius` is a measurement window with an overflow bin, not a
spatial boundary. Addition/removal of a positive and negative hop, addition/
removal of a phonon arc, endpoint shifts and external-time moves sample the
expansion. Forward and reverse proposal probabilities and the time-rescaling
Jacobian are included in the Metropolis ratios. Order caps are recorded; all
production runs had zero attempted cap violations.

At fixed scaled vertex positions, the external time has density proportional
to `tau^(Nh+2n) exp(-a tau)` on `[0,T]`. The sampler integrates this conditional
distribution into each time bin, including the corresponding energy estimator.
Normalization uses the exactly known zero-hop, zero-arc sector
`integral_0^T exp[(mu+U delta_i0) tau] d tau`.

For a source at the defect and sufficiently long time,
`G_00(tau) ~ Z0 exp[-(Edef-mu) tau]`. A correlated fit to the exact finite-bin
exponential yields `Edef` and the injection residue `Z0`.
Midpoint projectors separately measure:

* `p_i = <Psi_b|n_i|Psi_b>`: the full electron density, summed over all phonons.
* `Z_i = |<vac|c_i|Psi_b>|^2`: the local bound-state spectral pole weight. Its
  estimator requires the electron at `i` and no phonon line crossing the
  midpoint.
* `Zb = sum_i Z_i`: total phonon-vacuum probability. It is distinct from `p0`
  and from the injection residue `Z0`.
* `nph`: the number of phonon lines crossing the midpoint; `r_rms^2 = sum i^2 p_i`.

The reported binding energy uses two independently measured DiagMC energies:
`Delta_b = Eclean(k=0) - Edef`, with their statistical errors in quadrature.
VED values are provided separately. VED ground energies are used to choose the
auxiliary sampling shift `mu`; no reference energy or residue is imposed in the
DiagMC energy fit, midpoint measurements or spectral reconstruction.

## Verification and production data

The C++ kernel tests check proposal reversibility, locality, crossed topology,
residence times, time rescaling, conditional-energy integration and sampled path
invariants. Python tests check independent VED Hermiticity, the noninteracting
impurity energy and density, the atomic limit, local spectral moments,
Hellmann–Feynman derivatives, local normalization and synthetic continuation.

Five sampled analytic checks cover free hopping, the noninteracting impurity,
the atomic oscillator and two deliberately truncated expansions. An additional
finite-time atomic check validates the midpoint phonon-vacuum projector and
phonon number. It reuses pilot data and is not counted as new sampling.

`defect_reference.py` constructs an independent real-space variational basis:
the electron's absolute position and a relative phonon cloud. The cloud is
generated by creation at the electron and cloud shifts, as in the clean VED
basis, with independent cloud-generation and electron-position cutoffs.
Ground-state checks use 8, 10 and 12 generations, then increase the electron
radius by 8 sites. The production cases pass a conservative comparison screen
against the independent reference; this screen is not a rigorous error bound.

All three jobs completed on `highmem`, node `b000`, with exit code `0:0`:

| Stage | Job | Chains | Production steps | Elapsed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pilot and analytic checks | 20881037 | 28 | 128,000,000 | 00:00:31 |
| Six ground-state points | 20881090 | 48 | 1,536,000,000 | 00:05:52 |
| Local propagators and spectra | 20881164 | 72 | 1,152,000,000 | 00:05:55 |

Production ground runs have 8 independent seeds per parameter point, 320 blocks
of 100,000 steps per seed, and measurements every 100 steps. Each chain has
300,000 warm-up steps, excluded from the counts. Projection limits are
`T=128,64,32` for `U=0.5,1,2`. Three time windows and chain estimates are saved.
Statistical errors use block jackknifing with grouping factors 1, 2, 4 and 8,
and independent-chain jackknifing, retaining the largest estimated error.
Quoted errors do not incorporate every possible residual projection bias.

For `U=1`, ground energies are `-2.50638 +/- 0.00123` and
`-2.80218 +/- 0.00162` for `lambda=0.25,0.5`. Binding energies are
`0.27763 +/- 0.00124` and `0.33234 +/- 0.00163`. The larger coupling increases
localization at this fixed defect strength. Spatial exponential fits use
`p_i proportional to exp(-2|i|/xi)` in several finite windows. Tail counts and
window choice affect these fits, so `xi` is reported as a preliminary diagnostic,
not a precisely established asymptotic localization length.

## Local spectral map and its limits

For `U=1`, local propagators are sampled independently at sites `i=0,...,8`,
with four chains per site and coupling. Reflection symmetry supplies `-8,...,-1`:
there are 9 measured positions and 17 displayed positions per coupling.
The spectral map's horizontal axis is position, not a conserved momentum.
Off-diagonal propagators would be needed for a defect momentum-resolved matrix.

Positive continuation uses the exact time-bin kernel, block covariance and
held-out-chain cross-validation. The unbroadened local moments are
`M0=1`, `M1=V_i`, `M2=V_i^2+2t^2+g^2`. The lower support bound is
`-2t-U-g^2/omega`; the chosen upper support is `10t`. An energy grid with 321
points, 48 combined time bins (fitting up to `tau=8`), 14 candidate penalties,
and eight block bootstrap resamples are recorded. These finite numerical
choices introduce regularization and support uncertainties. The bootstrap
range is not a complete systematic error estimate.

Maps use Lorentzian HWHM `eta/t=0.25,0.5,1`. Before broadening each local
spectrum has unit total weight; the finite displayed window is not renormalized.
Local moments are checked before broadening, since Lorentzian high-order
moments are not appropriate for that check. The output shape is
`A[eta, site, energy] = (3,17,801)` with energy window `[-4.5,5.5]`.

Independent VED comparisons use all nine measured sites, 10 to 12 generations,
electron radius 24 to 40, and 200 to 400 Lanczos steps. The largest reference
changes at the tested broadenings are 0.21% for the cloud cutoff and 0.0078%
for radius. These are convergence differences, not rigorous upper bounds.

The **fine DiagMC spectral map remains preliminary**. Whole-spectrum relative
L1 errors against VED range from 11.4–44.0% (`lambda=0.25`) and 12.2–36.7%
(`lambda=0.5`) at `eta=0.25t`. At `eta=t` they decrease to 1.3–5.2% and
1.3–6.7%. The extra narrow peaks in the reconstruction cannot yet be assigned
to physical excitations. `spectral_resolution_validated` remains `false`.

## Reproduction

Requirements: C++17 compiler; Python with NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and pytest.
`scripts/python.sh` selects the existing cluster environment; elsewhere use
your Python interpreter with these packages. Run from the project root:

```sh
make all test
python -m pytest -q
python scripts/run_defect.py --stage pilot --out results/defect_pilot_01 --jobs 4
python scripts/run_defect.py --stage scan --pilot results/defect_pilot_01 --out results/defect_scan_01 --jobs 4 --chains 8 --blocks 320 --seed-offset 10000000
python scripts/defect_spectra.py --ground results/defect_scan_01 --out results/defect_spectra_01 --jobs 4 --blocks 160 --bootstrap 8
python scripts/report_defect.py --out results/defect_report_new
```

Use new output names or a fresh checkout: sampling and analysis directories
refuse overwrite. For the exact recorded Slurm runs, use
`scripts/defect_pilot.slurm`, `defect_scan.slurm`, and `defect_spectra.slurm` with
the account appropriate to your allocation. Their original logs, scheduler
accounting, source snapshots, executable hashes and parameter manifests are
included with the data. The clean-system DiagMC summaries used for binding are
included; their original full raw data remain in the earlier published packages.

Extract the main, both raw and both Green-function ZIP archives into one parent directory.
They share the `holstein-diagmc/` root and preserve `results/*/chains` paths.
The main archive includes current source, immutable run snapshots, reference
data, continuation output, reporting source and the clean binding summaries.
The two raw archives add pilot/ground chains and spectral chains respectively;
the two Green-function archives contain the normalized propagators and block arrays.
The ZIP files are hosted in the repository's `single-defect-20260923` release,
following the existing full-grid data distribution pattern.
`defect-archives.json` gives byte counts and SHA-256 digests.

To audit and regenerate the report from these archived results, run the last
report command above with an unused output directory. Original run snapshots
are retained for provenance even when current reporting code is improved.

The next useful accuracy work is longer independent-chain sampling and
explicit variation of time windows, energy support and continuation resolution
at a few selected sites, before assigning detailed spectral sidebands.
