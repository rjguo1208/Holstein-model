DiagMC Improvements and Validation

Zero temperature, one electron, infinite lattice. Updated: 2026-09-18.

View momentum–energy spectral maps: actual DiagMC reconstructions, independent VED references and accuracy checks.

The direct mass estimator substantially reduces statistical error. Spectral reconstruction now includes nonlinear pole fitting and stochastic optimization comparisons. Some parameter/method combinations still miss the 10% target, and fine spectral structure requires further validation.

The additional independent chains and postprocessing are complete. This page uses the combined long-imaginary-time results and new spectral data.

Direct mass estimation from momentum derivatives of diagrams

The existing samples retain derivative statistics associated with each electron momentum pip_ipi​ and propagation interval:

J=∑i2tsin⁡pi Δτi,K=∑i2tcos⁡pi Δτi.J=\sum_i2t\sin p_i\,\Delta\tau_i,\qquad K=\sum_i2t\cos p_i\,\Delta\tau_i.J=i∑​2tsinpi​Δτi​,K=i∑​2tcospi​Δτi​.

Fit the slope to obtain the inverse mass, retaining the intercept from the curvature of ZkZ_kZk​. The main window is τ∈[8,20]\tau\in[8,20]τ∈[8,20], with time bins merged to width 1. Exponential weighting within uniform time bins changes only the intercept of this asymptotic line; the plotted intercept is converted to the original bin width. For methodological background, see Ragni's direct estimator.

The statistical error is the largest estimate from three block lengths and a leave-one-chain-out jackknife. Time binning, covariance shrinkage and fitting windows are also varied. The first-round dispersion fits gave 1.1496 ± 0.0076 and 1.3543 ± 0.0161, consistent with the direct results.

Check imaginary-time data before assessing spectral reconstruction

Forward-transform the independent VED spectrum using the same exact bin-integrated kernel and compare it with the measured propagator. Neighboring time points from the conditional estimator are strongly correlated; their plotted errors are not independent. The analysis retains the data covariance.

An exact ground-state pole and two continuation methods

A(0,ω)=Z0δ(ω−E0)+Arest(0,ω).A(0,\omega)=Z_0\delta(\omega-E_0)+A_{\mathrm{rest}}(0,\omega).A(0,ω)=Z0​δ(ω−E0​)+Arest​(0,ω).

The updated Tikhonov method uses exact nonlinear profile optimization of the pole energy while retaining the correlated E0,Z0E_0,Z_0E0​,Z0​ errors from independent long-time sampling. At λ=0.25\lambda=0.25λ=0.25, the pole displacement changes from about −2.71σ-2.71\sigma−2.71σ to about −0.58σ-0.58\sigma−0.58σ.

The SOM variant represents a nonnegative spectrum with continuous rectangles, randomly changes their centers, widths and number, reoptimizes their areas, and averages several near-optimal solutions. It draws on the stochastic optimization approach of Mishchenko et al., but its updates and covariance-aware objective are not a step-by-step reproduction of that implementation. The spectral support allows bound excited-state weight above the ground state; it does not remove all weight below the one-phonon threshold in advance.

M0=1,M1=ε0,M2=ε02+g2.M_0=1,\qquad M_1=\varepsilon_0,\qquad M_2=\varepsilon_0^2+g^2.M0​=1,M1​=ε0​,M2​=ε02​+g2.

Both methods check the exact moments of the unbroadened spectrum. The regularization strength or optimization length is selected using held-out independent-chain predictions; the reference spectrum is not used for selection.

What resolution is actually supported?

Among the broadenings tested in this round, the smallest at which both methods and both couplings have incoherent-spectrum errors below 10% is η=0.6t\eta=0.6tη=0.6t. This is coarse frequency resolution and cannot establish individual phonon-sideband widths or fine peak structure. Increasing broadening does not meet the original η=0.25t\eta=0.25tη=0.25t target, and broadening is not a physical lifetime.

Additional sampling and subsequent calculations

The additional independent chains and postprocessing are complete. This page uses the combined long-imaginary-time results and new spectral data.

Automated postprocessing will combine the long-time data, repeat both continuation methods, perform block resampling and resolution checks, and record explicit acceptance results. Sampling and independent references at λ=0.75,1.0,1.5\lambda=0.75,1.0,1.5λ=0.75,1.0,1.5 will begin only after the stated mass target and the η=0.25t\eta=0.25tη=0.25t spectral target pass. Lower phonon frequencies and external phonon sectors are later stages; these calculations are not marked complete.

Validation includes independent momentum differences of diagram weights, mass extraction with a nonzero intercept, double integration of rectangular spectra, exact poles and spectral moments, broadening normalization, and the free-electron limit of stochastic optimization.
